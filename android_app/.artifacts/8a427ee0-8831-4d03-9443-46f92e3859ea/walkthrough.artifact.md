## How to Install the App on your Phone

Since I am an AI, I cannot send you the file directly, but you can generate it in **1 minute** using these steps:

1.  In your **Android Studio** (on your laptop), look at the top menu.
2.  Go to **Build** -> **Build Bundle(s) / APK(s)** -> **Build APK(s)**.
3.  Wait for the progress bar at the bottom to finish.
4.  Once finished, a small box will appear at the bottom right. Click **"Locate"**.
5.  **Copy the `app-debug.apk`** file to your phone (via WhatsApp, Email, or USB).
6.  Open the file on your phone and tap **Install**.
